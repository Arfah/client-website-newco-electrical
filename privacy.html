Privacy policy

For client review before launch. This is a standard UK GDPR template written for a site that collects no personal data directly. Check the business details and the date, then remove this notice.

Last updated: September 2026.

Newco Electrical is a sole trader business based in Stoke Poges, Buckinghamshire. This policy explains what happens to your personal information when you use this website or get in touch.

What this website collects

Nothing directly. This website has no contact forms, no user accounts and no analytics that identify you. It does not set cookies of its own.

Third-party services on this site

  • Google Maps. The contact section embeds a map from Google. When it loads, Google may set cookies and collect information about your visit under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and to keep the service running.

When you contact me

If you call, message me on WhatsApp or email me, I will hold the details you give me, usually your name, phone number, email address, property address and what the job involves, for as long as I need them to quote for and carry out the work, and afterwards for as long as I need to keep records for tax, certification and guarantee purposes.

The legal basis for this is that the information is needed to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and to carry out that contract. I do not sell your details or pass them to anyone else for marketing.

WhatsApp and Gmail are provided by Meta and Google respectively, and messages sent through them are subject to those companies' own privacy policies.

Electrical certificates

Where the work is notifiable, I am required to lodge the certificate with my certification body, NICEIC, and with the local authority building control. This means your name and address are shared with them as part of the certification process.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to how it is used. To do any of that, email newco.electrical85@gmail.com or call 07875 535811.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
